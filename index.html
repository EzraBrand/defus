Defus דפוס

Prototype

Open scans of printed Hebrew books and Judaica, 1469–1920, gathered from the libraries that hold them.

editions by year of printing

Editions

This prototype shows a sample of editions from an inventory of over 100,000 records harvested from HathiTrust, the Internet Archive, the Bavarian State Library (MDZ), UB Frankfurt and the National Library of Israel (harvests still running). Copies of one edition are grouped by shared catalogue numbers and by matching title, place and year; these groups are first-pass and may be wrong. The best copy is the one with open IIIF images, open rights and the highest resolution. Cover images load directly from the Internet Archive and the Bavarian State Library. Data: CC0 catalogue records of the holding libraries. Code and data: github.com/EzraBrand/defus.
